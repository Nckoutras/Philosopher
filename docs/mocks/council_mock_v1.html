Your matter

"I keep starting things and abandoning them before they're done. I'm afraid that if I commit to one path, I'll choose wrong."

The council deliberates…
M
Machiavelli
You scatter your forces and call it keeping options open. It is fear wearing prudence's mask. A prince who garrisons every town defends none — choose one ground and fortify it. The others were never yours to hold.
"It is better to be feared than loved, if you cannot be both."
The Prince
E
Epictetus
Whether you finish is in your power; whether it succeeds is not. You have confused the two, and abandon the first to escape the second. Do the part that is yours — complete one thing. Leave the rest to fortune, where it has always lived.
"Some things are within our power, and some are not."
Enchiridion
F
Freud
Notice what the abandoning protects. So long as a thing is unfinished, it cannot be judged — and neither can you. You leave before you can be left. The fear is not of choosing wrong; it is of being seen and found wanting.

Where the council stands

They name different causes — scattered force, a confusion of control, a flight from judgment, a refusal of responsibility — but converge on one verdict: the problem is not which path, but the unwillingness to commit to any. Finish one thing. Let it be judged.
